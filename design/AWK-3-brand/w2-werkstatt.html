askwerk · Werkstatt

Engineered, exact, a little industrial. Surveys as instruments: everything measured, labelled and in its place. Sharp corners, visible structure, monospaced figures, one signal colour that only marks what matters.

01 · Logo

A square tile with an angular question mark built from straight strokes, like a mark punched into sheet metal. The wordmark is set in condensed type; “werk” carries the signal colour. The tile alone is the favicon and app icon.

03 · Colour

Warm paper and graphite carry the interface; signal orange is used sparingly, for the one action or state that matters on a screen. Steel blue for links and information keeps orange free for emphasis. Light and dark values are tuned separately, not inverted.

04 · Shape and structure

Corners 2 px on controls and 4 px on cards; borders instead of shadows; a faint 24 px grid behind canvases. The signature detail is the measuring rule: progress and rating scales read like a ruler with ticks.
