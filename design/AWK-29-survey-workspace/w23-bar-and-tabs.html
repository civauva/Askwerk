W23 · The survey's bar and tabs, second round · AWK-29 · 2026-10-15

You rejected all five options of the first round. They all moved the same two buttons around. This round starts from why today's bar feels upside down, and then changes what goes where, not just where it sits. Each option is shown in three states: a draft while you edit it; a published survey with changes, seen from Results; and a phone.

Why today feels inverted

  1. The top row mixes which survey (name, status) with what to do now (Preview, Publish).
  2. The tabs mix two kinds of work: making the survey (Build, Settings) and after it is out (Share, Results, Participants). Publish is the bridge between them, but it floats above both.
  3. Preview is part of making a survey, not a command for the whole survey.

Rules every option below follows

  1. Top to bottom goes from general to specific: the survey, then where you are, then the tools for that place.
  2. An action sits next to the thing it acts on.
  3. The status says what Publish would do ("2 changes not published"), so the button is never a guess.

1 · Places above, tools below

The tabs move up into the survey's row, so it reads like a menu: this survey and its places. Below it is a tool row that belongs to the open tab, so its buttons change with the place. Publish appears in the tool row of Build and Settings. On Results, the tool row holds Filter and Export. This is the usual order of desktop tools: menus first, then the toolbar for the open document.

2 · Publish lives in "Edit"

Build and Settings become one tab, Edit. Inside it, a small switch chooses Questions, Settings or Preview, and Publish closes that row as the last step. The main tabs are then Edit · Share · Results · Participants, all on the same level: the four things you do with a survey.

3 · One "next step" button that follows the survey's state

The bar keeps today's tabs, but its button always shows the survey's next step, worded for the state it is in: a draft shows "Publish", a published survey with changes shows "Publish 2 changes", a published survey without changes shows "Copy link", and a closed survey shows "Reopen". Preview leaves the bar and becomes a mode of the canvas (Edit / Preview). The bar then holds identity plus exactly one action, and that action is never wrong for the tab you are on.
