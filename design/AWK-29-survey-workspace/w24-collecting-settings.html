D · Behaviour

  • Closing date is stored in UTC, entered and shown in this Askwerk's time zone (set in setup; organisations have none of their own yet). The runner refuses new and unfinished responses from that moment, even before the background close runs.
  • A background job closes the survey at the date, so the list and Results show "Closed"; audit entry survey.closed by the system, reason "closing-date".
  • Closing by hand before the date keeps the date; reopening removes a date that has passed.
  • Audit: survey.closing-date-set (old → new), survey.closing-date-removed, survey.one-response-changed (on/off).
  • One response per person (default on, also for existing surveys): the server refuses a second start from a browser whose response is complete (409 "already-answered"); the browser keeps its cookie for a year after finishing. Off: the end page offers "Answer again", which starts a new response.
  • Honest limit, in the note and the docs: it is per browser; another browser or a private window can answer again. For a strict one per person, use personal invitations.
  • Data: two columns on surveys (closes_at, one_response_per_person); the table's row-level security policy covers them, no new table.
